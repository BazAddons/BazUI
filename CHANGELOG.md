## 024

Updated for the World of Warcraft: Forever launch.

- Fixed bags failing to open with **B** or `/bbg` after the client changed how bag slots are initialized.
- Fixed the player casting bar moving in visible steps, especially after the computer has been running for several days.
- The default skin now uses **Flat** bar fills, removing the thick black gap inside the border. HUD fills remain available in Skin settings.
- Removed the Codex **Quests** catalog tab and its bundled database. The Today page still shows quests from your current log, and the Drawers quest tracker is unchanged. Old catalog caches are cleared automatically.
- Updated retail compatibility metadata for 12.1.5 and development paths for the live Forever client.
